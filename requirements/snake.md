# Snake

A browser snake game.

- A 20 x 20 grid. The snake starts with 3 segments in the middle, moving right.
- The arrow keys or WASD change direction. The snake cannot reverse straight back on itself.
- One food item is always on the board, placed randomly on a cell the snake does not occupy.
- Eating food grows the snake by one segment and scores 10 points. The snake speeds up a little every
  5 food items.
- Hitting a wall or the snake's own body ends the game. Show the final score and a Restart button.
- Keep the best score for the session and show it next to the current score.
- Space pauses and resumes the game.
