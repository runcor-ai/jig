# Pong

A browser Pong game, one player against the computer.

- Two paddles and a ball on a fixed-size playfield. The player moves the left paddle with W/S or the arrow keys.
- The computer moves the right paddle. It should be beatable but not trivial: limit its speed, and let it
  react a little late.
- The ball bounces off the top and bottom walls and off the paddles. Where it hits the paddle changes the
  angle, and it speeds up slightly with each paddle hit.
- A point is scored when the ball passes a paddle. The first to 10 wins.
- Show both scores, a "press Space to start" message before play, and a winner message with a Restart
  button at the end.
- Space pauses and resumes the game.
