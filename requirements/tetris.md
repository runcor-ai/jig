# Falling blocks

A browser falling-block puzzle game. Use original visuals and naming, not a copy of any commercial game's
look.

- A 10 x 20 grid. The seven standard four-square piece shapes fall one at a time, chosen randomly.
- Left/right arrows move the piece, Up rotates it, Down drops it faster, and Space drops it straight to the
  bottom.
- Pieces lock when they land. Full rows clear, and the rows above move down.
- Score: 100 / 300 / 500 / 800 points for clearing 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 rows at once. The level goes up every 10
  rows cleared, and pieces fall faster with each level.
- Show the next piece, the score, the level and the rows cleared.
- The game ends when a new piece cannot be placed. Show the final score and a Restart button.
- P pauses and resumes the game.
